Estimation controls

Groups are formed by exact item + category + rarity + upgrade + normalized enchantments + normalized substats. Within each group, the median and median absolute deviation (MAD) identify unusually distant values. For zero MAD, an IQR fallback is used when possible. Small groups (under five samples) are not automatically trimmed. The displayed estimate is the arithmetic mean of retained observations, with the median also shown in details. A flagged price is never deleted from history.

Backup & restore

This single-file version saves to your browser's localStorage. It is not cloud-synced. Export backups regularly; clearing browser data may erase your ledger. Restoring a backup replaces existing data.
